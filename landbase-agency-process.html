How We Support Your Agency Process

A practical 4-step engagement — from understanding your current flow to implementing clearer systems.

01

Discovery

We review how your agency currently handles applicant intake, endorsements, and documentation from first contact to deployment.

02

Process Map

Document each step of your workflow — who does what, when, and what information needs to move between people and stages.

03

Gap Review

Identify bottlenecks, missing handoffs, and areas where applicant movement slows down or documentation gets lost.

04

Implement

Apply recommended process changes, update tracking tools, and walk the team through the new workflow structure.

Common Questions

Do you work with new agencies just starting out?

Yes. We can help you set up the process from scratch or fix an existing workflow that's creating bottlenecks as you scale.

Do you handle POEA or DOLE documentation?

We provide workflow guidance and process structuring. For legal compliance documentation specific to POEA/DOLE, we recommend working with an accredited agency lawyer.

Can you train our recruitment staff?

Yes. As part of implementation, we walk through the new process with your team and can create simple reference guides for daily use.
