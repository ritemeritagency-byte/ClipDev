The 100+ Applicants Framework

Khalid Agency: Building 100+ Daily Online Applicants

Most agencies post jobs and wait. Khalid Agency took a different approach — every part of the acquisition path was rebuilt around what applicants actually needed to see before they would take action.

The landing page was restructured around a fee-free positioning and a clear job-seeker-first hierarchy. Ad creative was aligned to the real concern Filipino workers have before applying overseas. And the follow-up path was tied directly to a Google Sheets database so no lead went cold.

The result was consistent daily volume that the team could actually process because the intake system was built to handle it.

The 40+ Office-Show System

Rite Merit: Converting Leads into 40+ Office Shows

Traffic without conversion is just noise. Rite Merit already had applicant flow — the problem was that volume did not consistently translate into qualified office shows. Too many leads were getting lost between inquiry and appointment.

The fix was process discipline, not more ads. Qualification rules were tightened, a follow-up rhythm was built into the daily team workflow, and status tracking was moved into a structured Google Sheets dashboard so everyone could see where each applicant stood.

With that structure in place, the agency stopped chasing leads that were never ready and started focusing effort on the people who were. Office-show numbers improved and team operations scaled alongside it.
