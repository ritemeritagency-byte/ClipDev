Common Questions

Do I need Google Workspace?

A free Google account is enough. Google Workspace adds sharing and permission controls but isn't required to get started.

Can multiple people use the sheet?

Yes — the tracker is built so team members can update their own rows, and managers get a summary view of the full pipeline.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. We provide a revision window after handover and can update columns, dropdowns, or layouts as your process evolves.
